# Portfolio content audit

Date: 2026-09-21
Purpose: record the public claims and their limits in `src/data/portfolio.ts`. Project source repositories remain read-only references.

## Positioning

The site keeps “Engineering the structure behind serious products.” as the English hero and uses a natural Portuguese adaptation, “A estrutura por trás de produtos que importam.” The supporting principle remains “Complexity deserves an interface.” / “Complexidade merece uma interface à altura.” The hero carries role and positioning; project evidence carries technologies and implementation details.

The three cases support distinct emphases:

| Project | Public emphasis | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K-Libra | Systems engineering across operational domains | Local contracts and architecture evidence; no claim of remote deployment, production certification or perfect tenant isolation |
| Vocalis | Private vocal-practice product plus applied MIR research | Product capabilities are separated from QA-only transcription experiments and proposed controlled evaluation |
| AI-SDLC | Governed agent execution and evaluation systems | Evaluation campaigns remain experimental; no benchmark score or quality certification is claimed |

## Claim controls

- K-Libra's domain map is explicitly conceptual and selected; it is not a deployment topology.
- Vocalis distinguishes F0 feedback from note transcription. The separation benchmark is presented as a research direction, not as a measured result. No F1, precision, recall or superiority claim is published.
- AI-SDLC describes governance, identity, persistence and score eligibility without turning fixture/campaign counts into a success metric.
- Technologies are a secondary selected stack. They do not establish sole authorship, production status or commercial impact by themselves.
- Case ownership is expressed as “My role” / “Minha atuação” and limited to product engineering, architecture and implementation responsibilities supplied for this portfolio.
- External links point to public product/profile destinations. No private tracks, stems, dataset contents, checkpoints, customer information or hidden evaluation material is included.

## Remaining evidence limits

- The public K-Libra ERP is a customer portal, not an unauthenticated demo; visitors cannot inspect its authenticated runtime.
- Vocalis experiments do not support a public quantitative performance claim or scientific-novelty claim in this case.
- AI-SDLC campaign counts and pass totals are deliberately omitted; the portfolio describes evaluation methodology without implying certification.
- No revenue, customer count, adoption, production uptime or business outcome is claimed.
